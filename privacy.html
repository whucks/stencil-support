STENCIL / Support

Privacy, plainly.

Effective September 28, 2026

Stencil is an offline word puzzle game made by SageKey Labs, LLC. The app does not collect personal data, use analytics, show advertisements, track you, or require an account.

Your puzzles and progress

Daily puzzles are prepared on your device from content included with the app. Stencil does not connect to a puzzle server or an AI service. Your progress, results, streaks, and preferences are stored locally on your device. They may be included in backups managed by your operating system and Apple account settings. SageKey Labs does not receive those backups.

Sharing is your choice

When you choose Share, Stencil passes a result summary to the destination you select through the system share sheet. That destination handles the shared information under its own privacy policy. Stencil does not automatically send your results anywhere.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Your mail provider also handles the message under its own policies.

Removing your data

Deleting Stencil removes its local app data. Device backups are managed separately through Apple's settings. There is no Stencil account or server profile to delete.

Children

Stencil does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no account, advertising, chat, or user-generated content service.

Questions and changes

Contact hello@sagekeylabs.ai. If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.

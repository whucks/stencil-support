STENCIL / Support

Five cuts. Ten words.

Stencil is a free daily word puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Cross out exactly one letter in every row and every column. Read the remaining letters across and down: every line must spell a word. You have four checks to solve the board.

When does the daily puzzle change?

At midnight in your device's local time zone. Everyone playing the same local date receives the same daily board. Stencil works offline; you do not need an account or an internet connection for new daily puzzles.

What counts as a check?

A check is used only for a complete, new cut pattern. An invalid arrangement or a pattern already checked does not cost a check. Undo and Clear change your cuts without returning checks already spent.

Can I keep playing?

Practice boards are available any time and can be restarted. Practice does not change your daily streak or statistics.

Where is my progress?

It is saved on your device. There is no Stencil account or automatic cross-device sync. Your operating system may include the data in device backups. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Is Stencil free?

Yes. There are no purchases, subscriptions, advertisements, or paid hints.

Need help?
